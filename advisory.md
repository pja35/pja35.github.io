Advisory engagements are independent and non-operational. Work is performed in a personal capacity, subject to applicable institutional conflict-of-interest policies. Confidentiality is respected in all engagements.

## Typical Clients

- Venture capital and deep-tech investment funds
- Corporate innovation and strategy teams
- Scientific advisory boards
- Expert networks

## Typical Deliverables

- Written technical assessment memoranda
- Risk and feasibility analysis
- Expert calls and briefings
- Long-term scientific advisory relationships
