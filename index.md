# Quantum Information Expertise

*Independent scientific due diligence and advisory services for investors, boards and institutions evaluating quantum technologies.*
{: .site-subtitle}

## Pablo Arrighi

I am a Senior Researcher (Research Director) at Inria, heading the [QuaCS](https://www.inria.fr/en/quacs) team on quantum computation structures. I hold a PhD from the University of Cambridge and have authored 70+ publications in quantum computing, quantum simulation, and computer science.

Alongside my research, I now offer independent advisory services -- applying two decades of scientific evaluation experience to help non-specialists assess quantum technology claims.

## What I Offer

- Technical due diligence on quantum technologies for investment decisions
- Scientific advisory for boards, deep-tech ventures, and corporate innovation teams
- Assessment of technology roadmaps, scalability claims and theoretical soundness
- Confidential expert consultations (via expert networks or directly)

For larger engagements, I can assemble dedicated groups of experts drawing on my academic network.

## Why Me

My research gives me the scientific depth. What makes me specifically suited to evaluation and due diligence is a long track record of *institutional scientific assessment*:

- **National research evaluation** -- Member of CoNRS Section 6, the committee that selects, promotes and awards CNRS researchers in computer science (2019--2021). Referee for ANR, Digitéo, SIRTEQ etc.
- **International grant referee** -- Evaluated research proposals for funding agencies in Switzerland (SNSF), Korea (NRF), Belgium (FNRS), Luxembourg (FNR), Chile (Fondecyt), Slovenia (ARIS), Italy (PRIN), Uruguay (ANII), and EU programmes (CHIST-ERA), among others
- **Lab and strategy evaluation** -- HCERES expert, evaluating laboratories and university research strategies
- **Private-sector R&D assessment** -- Atos/Fourier prize committee member, Crédit Impôt Recherche expert, evaluating whether company research qualifies for public support
- **Industry collaboration** -- Leader of a dual contract DGA-Astrid (2009--2010), Co-supervised a CIFRE thesis with Quandela (2022--2025), scientific collaboration with Quantinuum.
- **Strategic advisory boards** -- Executive committee member of the Quantum Saclay center (2020--), Member of the Scientific and Training Advisory Board (STAB) of the Quanta Institute at Aix-Marseille Université (2026--)
